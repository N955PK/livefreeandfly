ski/lease

Privacy policy

Effective November 16, 2026

ski/lease is a private tool for the members of one shared ski house. It keeps track of who is staying which nights, guests, and shared house bills. It is run by the owner of livefreeandfly.org. It is not a commercial service, and only people on the house’s member list can use it.

What we collect

  • From Google, when you sign in: your email address, your name, and your profile picture. We use your email address to confirm that you are on the member list. We do not get access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar, files, or anything else in your Google account.
  • What members enter: a display name, email address, phone number, an optional Venmo username, and an optional picture for each member.
  • House activity: the nights each member plans to stay, guest counts and notes, shared expenses, payments between members, and house details such as the address, door code, and Wi-Fi.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, to show members who is at the house, and to work out who owes whom. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it for anything unrelated to the house.

Who can see it

  • House members. Everyone on the member list can see and edit everything in the app, including other members’ contact details.
  • The person who runs the app, who can see the data in order to operate and fix it.
  • The services that run it. Data is stored with Supabase, a database hosting company. Sign-in is handled by Google. The page itself is served by GitHub Pages. Each handles data under its own terms.

Nobody else. People who are not signed in, or who sign in with an email that is not on the member list, see nothing.

Cookies and tracking

There are no ads, analytics, or tracking. The app stores a sign-in token in your browser so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it.

Payments

The app does not move money or handle payment details. A “Pay on Venmo” link opens Venmo, and the payment happens there under Venmo’s terms.

Keeping and deleting data

House data is kept for as long as the house uses the app. You can change your own details at any time in the app. To have your information removed, ask at the address below. Past expenses you were part of may be kept, without your contact details, so the other members’ balances stay correct.

Children

This app is for adults who are members of the house. It is not meant for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
