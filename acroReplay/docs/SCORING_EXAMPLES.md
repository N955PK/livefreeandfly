# How a figure gets its grade — worked scoring examples

A companion to [COACHING_PLAN.md](COACHING_PLAN.md) for grading the Primary figures by hand (and for checking
what the coach says). Rule numbers are from the **IAC Official Contest Rules 2025** unless marked CIVA
(FAI Section 6 Part 1 Appendix B). Both PDFs are in `docs/private/`.

## 1. The method in one paragraph

Start every figure at **10.0**. Grading begins the moment the aircraft leaves level flight and ends when it is
back in wings‑level horizontal flight on a box axis (26.1.9). Deduct as each fault appears, in **0.5‑point
steps** (26.1.4), for the *error*, never for the correction that follows (26.6). Any angular error is **1 point per
5°** (0.5 per 2.5°); an error that reaches **90°** is a **Hard Zero** (26.3.1c). If a rule gives no number, deduct
in proportion to the error but at least 0.5 (Quick Reference). Give the benefit of the doubt (26.5). When the
deductions add to 10 or more the grade is a **numeric 0.0**; HZ beats 0.0 if both apply (26.2.3). Every zero
needs a reason in the remarks (26.2.2).

Three things decide most Primary grades:

| Line | Judged on | So the coach measures |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal (and every looping segment) | **flight path** of the CG against the true horizon | GPS/INS track and altitude, wind‑corrected roundness |
| Vertical | **attitude**: zero‑lift axis 90° from horizontal | Hub pitch (ZLA = IMU axis by ruling) |
| 45° | **attitude**: ZLA ±45° from vertical, wind ignored | Hub pitch |

## 2. Examples straight from the rulebook

These are the worked examples the IAC rulebook itself gives (paraphrased, with the rule):

| Situation | Deduction | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Slow roll to 360° rotates 365° then corrects back | 1 pt (the 5° over‑rotation; the correction is free) | 28.20.2 ex. 1 |
| Half roll (180°) stops at 130°, then corrects to 165° | **10 pts** — a stop mid‑roll is graded as the full 50° error; 130° → 165° then adds 15° → grade 0.0 | 28.20.2 ex. 2 |
| Same roll, but the rotation *slows* at 130° instead of stopping, finishing at 165° | 4 pts: 1 for the rate change + 3 for the 15° final error | 28.20.2 ex. 2 |
| Full roll rotates 270°, stops briefly, then finishes | **HZ** — a 90° error | 28.20.2 ex. 3 |
| 180° roll: fast to 135°, dramatic slow‑down for the last 45°, finishes on angle | 1 pt (one rate variation) | 28.20.1 |
| One‑turn spin: autorotation stops at 345°, ailerons finish the last 15° | 3 pts | 28.24.7 |
| Spin entry: 10° of pitch and 10° of roll seen before any yaw | 4 pts (1 per 5° on each lagging axis) | 28.24.2 |
| Roll at a loop apex starts 5° before the apex and ends 15° after (mid‑point 5° off centre) | 1 pt | 27.12.3 |
| 4‑point roll: first point stops at 100°, second stops exactly at 180° | 2 pts for the first point only; the second is not charged for its misaligned entry | 26.6.2 |
| 45° up line with a full roll, aircraft returned to level immediately after the roll (no line after) | 4 pts | 27.9.4d (CIVA B.8.1.5) |
| Square loop: final horizontal line half the length of the first, then the next figure starts | 2 pts for the 1:2 line; because *some* final line was seen, no "no line between" deduction | 28.12.2 |
| Square loop: no final line seen before the next figure | 4 pts on the loop + 1 pt on the next figure | 28.12.2 |
| No discernible horizontal line between two figures | 1 pt on **each** figure | 26.7.1 |
| Competitor obviously above 3,500 ft and close in, figure can't be assessed | 2 pts (scorability) | 27.15.1 |

And one from British Aerobatics' "Marking the figures" page, showing how a sheet adds up: faults noted as the
figure progressed — 5° nose‑up at the start (1), 5–10° bank (1.5), 5° yaw and 10° roll off axis mid‑figure
(1 + 2), 5° yaw and 0–5° nose‑down at the exit (1 + 0.5): `10 − 1 − 1.5 − 1 − 2 − 1 − 0.5 = 3.0`.

## 3. Worked examples for the Primary Known figures

These are composed from the 2025 rules to show how typical faults on each Primary figure turn into a grade.
They are illustrations, not official cases.

### 3.1 45° up line (`d`, 1.1.2.1)

Judge watches: the ⅛ pull, the 45° attitude (ZLA), wings level, heading, the ⅛ push back to level, and that a
horizontal line is shown before and after.

| Fault seen | Deduction | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Line drawn at 50° (5° steep) | 1 | 27.4, 27.6 |
| Attitude shallows to 45° partway along the line | ≥1 (attitude changed on the line) | CIVA B.8.1.3 |
| Left wing 5° low on the line | 1 | 27.6 |
| Exit heading 5° right of the box axis | 1 | 26.1.8, 27.6 |
| **Grade** | **6.0** | |

Common trap: the line looks much steeper from the cockpit than it is. Judges see 40° lines constantly.

### 3.2 1½‑turn spin (`iv6s`, 1.1.6.3 + 9.11.1.6)

Judge watches: a horizontal line flown to the stall with the CG not rising; nose‑drop, yaw and roll starting
together; autorotation for 540°; rotation stopping precisely on the box axis; a vertical, wings‑level down line;
a constant‑radius pull to level.

| Fault seen | Deduction | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Nose pitched up 5° (CG rose) just before the break — "forced" | 1 | 28.24.2, CIVA B.9.29.3 |
| Yaw starts ~10° of pitch after the nose drops | 2 | 28.24.2 |
| Autorotation stops at 530°, ailerons finish the last 10° | 2 | 28.24.7 |
| Down line 5° past vertical (ZLA) | 1 | 27.3, 27.6 |
| Exit heading 5° off the axis after the pull | 1 | 28.24.6 |
| **Grade** | **3.0** | |

Not deductible: pitch attitude and rotation rate during the spin (28.24.5), wind‑blown entry appearance (28.24.3),
where the spin sits on the down line (28.24.9). Never stalls → **HZ** (28.24.4). Over‑rotates by 90° → **HZ**.

### 3.3 Half Cuban with half roll (`c2`, 8.5.2.1 + 9.1.3.2)

Judge watches: the ⅝ loop as a constant radius (quarter one sets it), stopping exactly at 45° inverted; the 45°
inverted line; the half roll centred (equal line before and after, by *length*); the 45° upright line; the ⅛
pull to level on heading.

| Fault seen | Deduction | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Loop tightens over the top (visible pinch, quarter 2) | 1 | 27.10.4 method 2 |
| Down line drawn at 35° (10° shallow) | 2 | 27.4, 27.6 |
| Line after the roll about half the length of the line before | 2 | 27.9.4b |
| Roll rate slows in the second half | 1 | 28.20.1 |
| Exit 5° off heading | 1 | 27.6 |
| **Grade** | **3.0** | |

Not deductible: the ⅛ exit radius differing from the ⅝ radius (28.20‑family rule: half Cuban radii need not
match, CIVA B.9.20.1); exit altitude different from entry.

### 3.4 Loop (`o`, 7.4.1.1)

Judge watches: constant radius through four quadrants, wind‑corrected (round as seen from the ground); wings
level; no crab; exit at entry altitude and on heading.

| Fault seen | Deduction | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Quarter 2 visibly tighter than quarter 1 (pinched top) | 1 | 27.10.4 method 2 |
| Quarter 4 flattens — radius about 1:2 vs quarter 1 ("e‑shape") | 2 | 27.10.4 method 2 |
| Right wing 5° low over the top | 1 | 27.6 |
| Exit 10° off heading | 2 | 27.6 |
| Exit ~80 ft below entry | 1 (proportional; no fixed value) | 27.10.3 + Quick Reference |
| **Grade** | **3.0** | |

Loop size is never a criterion (CIVA B.9.7.1). Sideways drift with the nose on axis is free (27.14.2).

### 3.5 180° competition turn (`2j`, 2.2.1.1)

Judge watches: a roll to ≥60° bank on the entry heading (a pause is allowed), a constant‑bank, constant‑altitude,
constant‑rate 180° heading change, a stop on the exit axis, then a roll out at the entry roll's rate.

| Fault seen | Deduction | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Bank established at 50° (10° short of 60) | 2 | 28.5.2 |
| Bank steepens 10° mid‑turn | 2 | 28.5.3 |
| Climbs ~100 ft during the turn | 1 | 28.4.5 |
| Roll‑out begins 10° of heading early | 2 | 28.5.5 |
| Roll‑out visibly slower than the roll‑in | 1 | 28.5.4 |
| **Grade** | **2.0** | |

Wind is never a factor in a turn (28.4.3); turn direction is the pilot's choice (28.4.4).

### 3.6 Slow roll (`1`, 1.1.1.1 + 9.1.3.4)

Judge watches: constant roll rate from start to finish, a crisp stop exactly at 360°, the CG track unchanged
(no climb, dive or heading change), horizontal lines before and after.

| Fault seen | Deduction | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Rate slows through the inverted quarter | 1 | 28.20.1 |
| Flight path sags ~5° while inverted | 1 | 28.20.3, 27.6 |
| Stops at 370° and rolls back | 2 (the over‑rotation; correction free) | 28.20.2, 26.6.1 |
| Finishes 5° off heading | 1 | 27.6 |
| **Grade** | **5.0** | |

A visible *stop* partway (e.g. at 270°) is graded as the angular error to that point — 90° → **HZ**.

## 4. IAC judges' revalidation exam (2025) — the scoring questions, worked

The exam is public (22 pages, 39 questions, each hinting the rule); the answer key is not. Answers below are
mine, from the 2025 text, and are marked *reading* where the rule leaves interpretation. Useful as drills.

| Q | Scenario | Answer & reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Snap over‑rotated 15°, momentary pause, aileron roll then finishes wings‑level having rotated only 345° | **3 pts** (A). The snap's 15° = 3. The pause is required between unlinked rolls (27.8.2). The aileron roll's 345° is the correction of the snap's error, not a new error (26.6.2). |
| 11 | Slight under‑rotation on roll 1, wings yawed 5° after roll 2, 10° over‑rotation on roll 3 — minimum | **3.5 pts** (B): 0.5 + 1 + 2 (26.1.4, 27.6.1). |
| 12 | Spin over‑rotated by 90° | **HZ** (26.3.1c). |
| 13 | 45° line 15° steep (3) + snap over‑rotated 25° (5) + finishes 20° off heading (4) = 12 | **0.0**, numeric (26.2.1a) — no single error reached 90°. |
| 14 | Spin that never autorotates | **HZ** (28.24.4). |
| 15 | Power tailslide slides less than ½ fuselage | **HZ** (28.9.3). (CIVA would give 4 pts — an IAC difference.) |
| 16 | Pulls straight from a down line into the next figure's 45° up line, no horizontal; figure still clearly visible far right | **A and B**: 1 pt from each figure for no line between (26.7.1) and a note for Presentation (29.3.1). Not the 2‑pt scorability deduction — it was seen clearly (27.15.1). |
| 19 | The zero‑lift axis is… | a function of the airfoil and angle of incidence (27.2.1); it does not change between upright and inverted. |
| 20 | 45° lines are judged by… | the ZLA relative to vertical, **wind ignored** (27.4). |
| 22 | Snap: nose pitches up, autorotates, but at 180° the tail stops corkscrewing and the roll finishes on‑axis to upright | **0.0** (A): properly initiated so not HZ, but autorotation ceased with 180° remaining → 36 pts (28.22.7). |
| 24 | Hammerhead: wings 5° off axis on the up line and stay so; backslide < ½ span before the pivot; clean pivot and down line | *Reading:* **1 pt** (A): the 5° once (26.6.2 hint), no charge for a backslide within the ½‑span allowance (28.8.3). |
| 25 | Humpty: perfect first quarter, pinched second quarter | **Your consistent method** (D) — no standardised radius deduction (27.10.4). |
| 27 | Square loop's last horizontal is half the first vertical, then the hammerhead starts | **2 pts** on the loop (C) — 1:2 line (27.9.4b); a final line *was* seen, so no "no line between" (28.12.2 clarification). |
| 28 | Immelmann with a long line between the half loop and the half roll | **Proportional to the error** (D): the pause must be short (27.11.2) and unspecified deductions are proportional (27.7.1). |
| 29 | Roll combination on a loop begins 15° before the apex, ends 25° after; pause 5° before apex | **1 pt** (A): the combination's midpoint is 5° off centre (27.12.3); the pause need not sit at the apex. |
| 30 | Inside‑outside eight, second radius half the first | **At least 0.5, applied consistently** (B) (27.13.4). |
| 31 | Heading 5° upwind of the Y axis while drifting downwind | **1 pt** for the heading (C) (27.14.1); the drift itself is not deductible (27.14.2). |
| 32 | Rolling turn: roll rate slows, speeds up, then stops momentarily | **At least 3 pts** (C): two variations (28.6.5) + a stop (28.6.6). |
| 36 | 4‑point roll slightly over‑rotated at all four stops; one hesitation longer than the first — minimum | **3 pts** (C): 4 × 0.5 + 1 (28.21.2, 28.21.4). |
| 37 | Snap with no pitch change seen, yaw then autorotation | **HZ** (B): both the pitch change and the autorotation must be seen (28.22.4). |
